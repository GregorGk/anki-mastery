🎧 What to listen for — BP vs EP audio cues
  • Final unstressed /e/ — BP palatalizes to [dʒi] / [tʃi] in words ending in -de, -te (e.g. verde = "verji", noite = "noytʃi"). EP keeps a clean [dɨ] / [tɨ] or near-silent schwa.
  • Unstressed /e/ everywhere else — EP reduces heavily to [ɨ] or omits it (pequeno → "pquenu"). BP keeps it as a clear [e] or [i].
  • Coda /s/ before consonant or pause — EP [ʃ] ("ash"), BP [s] ("ass"). fez: BP [fes], EP [feʃ].
  • /r/ at end of syllable — BP often retracted/velar/uvular (varies by region); EP usually a clean alveolar tap or trill.
  • /v/ — should be labiodental [v] in both. A bilabial [β] (Spanish-like) is a sign of foreign training data leaking into the model.
  • Lexical EP markers — sítio (= place, in EP), aldeia, concelho, comboio, autocarro, telemóvel. BP would use local/lugar, vila, município, trem, ônibus, celular.
