How to use this — and how the three IPA columns work together

Per clip, play the audio and decide whether the spoken pronunciation matches a textbook BP target. The three word-IPA columns give you three independent guesses at that target:

IPA columns

  • Stage 5 (LLM) — what's currently stored in 06-final.tsv, produced by an LLM during the IPA pass. Inconsistent on tricky cases, hence this review.
  • MFA dict — portuguese_brazil_mfa.dict (34,978 entries). Curated for the Montreal Forced Aligner's BP acoustic model. Multiple variants are joined with / . Empty if the word is OOV (out of vocabulary).
  • espeak pt-br — phonemizer + espeak-ng, rule-based, deterministic. Quirks normalized to Stage-5 convention (y→i, x→ʁ, lj→ʎ, …).

Decision heuristic

  • All 3 agree + audio matches → good
  • All 3 agree but audio diverges → bad (mispronunciation)
  • The 3 disagree → this row was always going to be controversial. Note it.
